# Mission Reflection

This laboratory helped me understand cloud computing in a more practical way because I was able to compare AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud instead of only studying their definitions. Among the three platforms, AWS impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and flexibility. I also learned that Azure is especially useful for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud is strong in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three platforms provide virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, databases, containers, security, and monitoring services. They also use global infrastructure with regions and zones to help organizations deploy applications around the world. Even though their service names and interfaces are different, their basic cloud computing functions are often similar.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. For example, a startup may prioritize cost and scalability, while a university using Windows Server and Microsoft 365 may benefit more from Azure. An AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI and machine learning services. A global e-commerce company may prioritize availability, scalability, and worldwide infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. A consultant must understand the client's current technology, budget, security requirements, workload, users, and future plans before making a recommendation.

This mission also improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio. My repository now contains another complete laboratory that includes research, comparisons, business recommendations, Linux investigation, screenshots, and reflection. It shows my progress in understanding cloud computing and applying it to realistic business situations. Overall, this activity helped me become more confident in comparing cloud technologies and thinking like a beginner Cloud Solutions Consultant.
